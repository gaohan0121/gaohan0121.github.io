My research lies at the intersection of multimodal learning, remote sensing, and intelligent agents. I am particularly interested in building models that connect heterogeneous visual observations with language and structured reasoning.

## Multimodal large language models for remote sensing

I study how large language models can be connected with hyperspectral, optical, and SAR imagery. The goal is to enable richer semantic understanding while preserving the spectral and spatial information needed for scientific remote sensing tasks.

Current topics include:

- hyperspectral image change detection;
- vision-language alignment for remote sensing imagery;
- instruction-driven interpretation of multi-temporal observations;
- reliable evaluation of multimodal remote sensing models.

## Cross-modal representation learning

I explore shared representation spaces for heterogeneous sensors, with a current focus on retrieval between SAR and RGB/optical imagery. My work investigates CLIP-style alignment, contrastive learning objectives, structural feature extraction, and cross-modal feature fusion.

## Agentic AI

I am also interested in tool-augmented and multi-agent systems for research workflows. This includes retrieval, evidence analysis, synthesis, and coordination among specialized agents using technologies such as MCP, A2A, and retrieval-augmented generation.

## Research principles

Across these directions, I value clear problem formulation, careful data curation, reproducible experiments, and evaluation that reflects real scientific use cases.
