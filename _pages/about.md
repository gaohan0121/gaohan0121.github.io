# Gaohan Gao <span lang="zh-CN">（高晗）</span>

**Undergraduate Student · Software Engineering**<br>
**Xi'an Jiaotong University**

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Software Engineering at Xi'an Jiaotong University.

My research interests focus on multimodal artificial intelligence, including multimodal large language models, remote sensing foundation models, vision-language learning, and agentic AI.

During my undergraduate study, I have conducted research under the supervision of Prof. Xiangyong Cao, working on multimodal learning and intelligent interpretation of remote sensing data.

My current research explores multimodal large language models for hyperspectral image change detection, cross-modal retrieval between SAR and optical images, and agent-based intelligent systems.

## Research interests

- Multimodal Large Language Models
- Remote Sensing Foundation Models
- Vision-Language Learning
- Hyperspectral Image Change Detection
- SAR-RGB Cross-modal Retrieval
- Agentic AI and Multi-Agent Systems
