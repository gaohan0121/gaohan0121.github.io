This project reformulates hyperspectral change detection from pixel-level classification into a multimodal semantic understanding task. The work builds question-answering data from multi-temporal hyperspectral observations and investigates instruction-tuned multimodal models that jointly identify changed regions, recognize change categories, and provide semantic explanations.

## Research topics

- Multimodal Large Language Models
- Remote Sensing Intelligence
- Change Detection
- Vision-Language Alignment

## Contributions

- Preprocessed multi-temporal hyperspectral data and refined ground-truth annotations.
- Designed an automated pipeline for region-level captions and question-answer pairs.
- Implemented and adapted multimodal models using SFT and PEFT methods.
- Conducted experimental evaluation, performance analysis, and paper writing.
