This project develops an intelligent research assistant for paper discovery, ranking, question answering, novelty analysis, literature synthesis, and knowledge-base synchronization.

## Agent architecture

- **Retrieval Agent:** discovers and prioritizes relevant literature.
- **Analysis Agent:** extracts structured notes, reviews methods, and tracks research trends.
- **Generation Agent:** produces evidence-grounded summaries and literature reviews.

## Technologies

- MCP and A2A
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation
- Tool-augmented agents
- Zotero and Obsidian integration
- Agent tracing and workflow observability
