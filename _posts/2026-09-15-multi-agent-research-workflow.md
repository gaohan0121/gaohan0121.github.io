## Workflow

The prototype separates retrieval, analysis, and generation into specialized agents connected through a shared research workflow.

## Components

1. A retrieval agent collects relevant papers and metadata.
2. An analysis agent extracts methods, datasets, and findings.
3. A generation agent produces a structured research summary.
